Privacy Policy

KASTMOR designed Kwelipa as an offline game that respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Effective date: 7 October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Kwelipa, developed by KASTMOR. Kwelipa is designed for offline play and does not require you to create an account or log in.

Privacy at a glance: Kwelipa does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not request device permissions and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

2. Information We Collect

Kwelipa does not collect personal information or personal data from users. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, identifiers, or other personal information through the app.

3. Accounts and Sign-In

Kwelipa does not require account registration, sign-in, or login. You can use the game without providing account credentials or profile information.

4. Offline Use

The game is designed to work offline. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection.

5. Local Game Data

Kwelipa may store game-related information locally on your device, such as progress, preferences, or high scores, so the game can function and remember your progress. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to KASTMOR.

You can generally remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's operating-system behavior and backup settings.

6. Permissions

Kwelipa is designed not to request access to sensitive device features or personal information. The app does not require permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access for gameplay.

7. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Kwelipa does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, behavioral tracking, or third-party tracking technologies in the app. KASTMOR does not sell or share user information for advertising purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

The app is designed to operate without third-party online services for its core functionality. If a future version introduces a service that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

9. Children's Privacy

Kwelipa is intended for users aged 13 and above. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from users, including children. If you believe a privacy issue involving a child has occurred, please contact KASTMOR using the details below.

10. Data Security and Retention

Because KASTMOR does not collect or receive personal data through Kwelipa, we do not maintain a server-side database of Kwelipa user personal information. Game data stored locally is subject to the security and data-management features of your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Kwelipa's features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. When we make changes, we will revise the effective date shown above. We encourage users to review the current policy when a new app version materially changes its data practices.
